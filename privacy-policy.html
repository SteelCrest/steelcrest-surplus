SMS Notification Program
Effective DateMay 2, 2025 Last UpdatedMay 2, 2025 Applies ToSMS Alert System

Overview

SteelCrest Surplus LLC ("SteelCrest," "we," "us," or "our") operates an internal automated SMS notification system used exclusively by the business owner to receive deal alerts and operational updates from our retail arbitrage scanning platform.

This SMS program sends messages only to the business owner. No messages are sent to customers, the public, or any third parties.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information solely for the purpose of delivering internal SMS notifications:

Mobile phone number — entered by the business owner in the SteelCrest internal dashboard to receive automated system alerts.

We do not collect personal information from any third parties, customers, or members of the public.

How We Use Your Information

The mobile phone number collected is used exclusively to send automated SMS alerts to the business owner, including:

Deal opportunity notifications, purchase confirmations, listing status updates, and daily system scan summaries. This information is not used for marketing, advertising, or any purpose beyond internal operational notifications.

Consent & Opt-In

The business owner provides explicit consent to receive SMS messages by entering their mobile phone number into the SteelCrest internal dashboard and checking a consent checkbox at account setup.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on deal scanning activity.

Opt-Out Instructions

To stop receiving SMS messages at any time, reply STOP to any message. You will receive a confirmation and no further messages will be sent.

To re-enable notifications, reply START. For help, reply HELP or contact us at the address below.

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your mobile phone number or any personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Your phone number is transmitted to Twilio, our SMS delivery provider, solely for the purpose of delivering messages. Twilio's privacy practices are governed by their own privacy policy at twilio.com/legal/privacy.

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. Your phone number is stored in a secured database accessible only to SteelCrest systems.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the SMS notification system after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
